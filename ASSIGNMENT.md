# Week-06-Red-Team-the-Claim-Audit-the-Model
Week 06 — Red Team the Claim, Audit the Model: Contamination, Fairness, and an AI RMF Risk Register
Week 06 | assessment | CLO4 CLO5 CLO6
Scenario
You are an AI Governance Analyst at Meridian Health Plan, a regional insurer piloting an assistant that drafts member-appeal summaries for utilization-review staff. Chief Risk Officer Daniel Okafor has two asks before the pilot gate. First, red-team one recent frontier research claim of your choosing — an emergent-ability, reasoning-benchmark, or agentic-capability claim from arXiv or a public lab technical report — identifying the measurement design, what the evidence genuinely supports, and where benchmark contamination or eval-set leakage could explain the result. Second, audit the small adapted model you built in Week 3 (or a comparable small adapted model you fine-tune yourself): run a self-authored safety probe suite, evaluate fairness across at least two slices of a synthetic member population you generate yourself with Python or Claude Code, and test for contamination between your training and evaluation data using n-gram overlap or an inserted canary. Build the risk register spreadsheet in Excel or Google Sheets, mapping every finding to a NIST AI RMF function and to relevant ISO/IEC 42001 clauses with a named mitigation, an owner, and a residual-risk judgment. Then brief Daniel on camera, prioritized the way a risk officer needs to hear it — top risk first, not chronological.
Requirements
•	Camera-on video solution walkthrough (5-10 min) + solution artifacts: a risk register spreadsheet mapped to NIST AI RMF functions and ISO/IEC 42001 clauses, and an audit notebook containing your safety prompt suite, slice evaluation, and contamination check
Video Submission Requirements
•	Maximum duration: 10 minutes
•	Format: MP4 (preferred), MOV, or WebM
•	Camera: Your face must be visible on camera for the entire video. Picture-in-picture overlay during screen-share is acceptable, but voice-over slides without a visible face is not permitted.
•	Script policy: Explain in your own words. Reading verbatim from a written script is not permitted — a brief outline or speaker notes for structure is fine.
•	Audio: Clear narration — use a quiet space and speak toward the microphone
•	Upload: Submit via the LMS assignment dropbox
Recording tools (free): Zoom (local recording), Loom, OBS Studio, or your device's built-in screen recorder.
